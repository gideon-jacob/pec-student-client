# Feature: Timetable Management

## Purpose

The Timetable Management feature is designed to help you, the student, easily view your daily and weekly class schedule. Its main goal is to keep you organized by providing a clear overview of your classes, so you always know what's next and can quickly access details about each subject.

Key benefits include:
*   **Staying Organized:** See your entire day's schedule at a glance.
*   **Planning Ahead:** Quickly check upcoming classes for any day of the week.
*   **Quick Subject Info:** Access essential details like subject name, code, type, assigned faculty, and your attendance percentage for that subject directly from the timetable view.

## User Interaction / UI Flow

### Accessing the Timetable
The timetable is the central part of your **Home** page, which is the first screen you see after logging in. There's no separate navigation needed to find your current day's schedule.

### Viewing Different Days
*   Above the subject list, you'll find a **weekday navigation bar** (Sunday, Monday, Tuesday, etc.).
*   Simply click on any day to view the class schedule for that specific day. The currently selected day will be highlighted.

### Understanding Subject Cards
Each class scheduled for the selected day is displayed as a "Subject Card." Each card provides the following information:
*   **Subject Name:** The full name of the course (e.g., "Database Management Systems").
*   **Subject Code:** The official code for the subject (e.g., "CS23401").
*   **Subject Type:** Indicates if it's a "Theory," "Laboratory," or "Activity" class.
*   **Faculty Name:** The name of the faculty member(s) teaching the course.
*   **Attendance Percentage:** Your current attendance percentage for that specific subject.
*   **Slot Number:** The period or slot number for that class.

### Interacting with Subject Cards
Currently, clicking on a subject card might highlight it or expand it slightly to show it's selected (this interaction may vary as the app evolves). The primary purpose is to clearly display the information for each scheduled class.

### No Classes Today?
If there are no classes scheduled for a selected day (e.g., on a Sunday or a holiday), the timetable area will display a friendly message like "No Classes Today" along with an icon, so you're clearly informed.
